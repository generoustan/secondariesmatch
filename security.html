Security, Compliance & Data Integrity

Built to survive counsel review before a single data room opens.

Every control below exists because a General Counsel reads this page before their fund is allowed to view a single listing. This is the detail behind the four-item summary on the homepage.

Bank-grade encryption

Data room documents are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Encryption keys are managed separately from document storage, and access requires an authenticated, logged session.

Verified counterparties

Every buy-side and sell-side member completes KYC/AML screening and accreditation verification before receiving marketplace access. No member transacts anonymously.

Immutable audit trail

Every listing view, NDA execution, data-room access, and bid is timestamped and permanently logged — the same record is available to both counterparties and, on request, their counsel.

Watermarked data rooms

Diligence documents are individually watermarked per viewer and per view. A leaked page can be traced back to the specific member session that opened it.

Access review & session controls

Institutional accounts support multi-seat access with role-based permissions (analyst, MD, compliance, view-only) and admin visibility into every seat's activity.

Data residency & sub-processors

Diligence data is held in region-appropriate infrastructure. A current sub-processor list is available on request during onboarding review.
