1. Information We Collect

  • Identity & entity data — name, title, firm, and documentation submitted for KYC/AML and accreditation verification.
  • Platform activity — listings viewed, filters saved, bids submitted, and data-room access, all tied to the audit trail described in our Security page.
  • Listing content — mandate and pricing information submitted by sellers for marketplace display, per each listing's disclosure settings.

2. How We Use It

Data is used to verify eligibility, operate the marketplace and deal-room workflow, maintain the audit trail members and their counsel rely on, and communicate about matters relevant to a member's account or an active transaction.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

Identity and diligence data may be shared with verification and KYC/AML vendors, with the specific counterparty a member is transacting with (limited to what that transaction requires), and where legally compelled. Listing data marked confidential is disclosed only to buyers who have executed the applicable NDA.

4. Data Room Activity

Access to and viewing of documents within a data room is logged, watermarked, and retained as part of the platform's audit trail, and is visible to the listing owner and, where applicable, to compliance reviewers on either side of the transaction.

5. Retention

Account and transaction records are retained for the period required to support closed-deal recordkeeping and applicable regulatory obligations, after which they are archived or deleted per a retention schedule to be finalized by counsel.

6. Your Rights

Depending on jurisdiction, members may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, subject to the platform's recordkeeping obligations for closed transactions. Requests can be directed to the contact below.

7. Security

Technical and organizational safeguards for member data are described in full on the Security & Compliance page.

8. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be communicated to active members in advance of taking effect.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: privacy@secondariesmatch.com.
