Risk Disclosures

Secondary transactions in private fund interests and private company securities involve substantial risk and are suitable only for institutional and accredited investors.

1. Illiquidity

Interests transacted through SecondariesMatch are not listed on any national securities exchange and are illiquid. There is no guarantee a member will be able to resell a position, at any price, in the future.

2. Loss of Principal

Buyers may lose some or all of their invested capital. Sellers may transact at a discount to reported NAV or last-round valuation that does not reflect the ultimate realized value of the underlying assets.

3. Pricing & Discounts

Reported NAV, ask, and indicative bid figures reflect the counterparties' own representations and market conditions at a point in time. They are not independently audited by SecondariesMatch and may not reflect current fair value.

4. Transfer Restrictions

Many fund interests and company securities are subject to transfer restrictions, rights of first refusal (ROFR), and GP or company consent requirements disclosed per listing. A transaction may fail to close if consent is withheld.

5. No Guarantee of a Match

SecondariesMatch operates a matching marketplace. Listing an interest does not guarantee a buyer will be found, and submitting a bid does not guarantee a seller will accept it.

6. Suitability

Access is restricted to institutional and accredited investors who have represented that they understand and can bear the risks described here. This page is not investment advice as to whether any specific transaction is suitable for a particular member.

7. Valuation Benchmarks

Indicative pricing benchmarks shown to sellers during listing intake are estimates for reference only, not a firm valuation, appraisal, or guarantee of achievable price.

8. Conflicts of Interest

SecondariesMatch and its origination team may earn fees from either or both sides of a transaction and may prioritize outreach on certain mandates. Specific conflicts-of-interest policy to be finalized by counsel.

9. No Investment Advice

Nothing on this platform is investment, legal, or tax advice. Members should consult their own advisors before transacting.
