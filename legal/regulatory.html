2. Broker-Dealer Network

Where transactions require broker-dealer involvement, they are executed through a registered broker-dealer network member. That member's name, CRD number, and the scope of services it provides will be disclosed here once confirmed.

3. Jurisdictions Served

Access is limited to eligible institutional counterparties in permitted jurisdictions. A jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction eligibility list (and any excluded jurisdictions) is to be maintained here and enforced at onboarding.

4. Not a National Securities Exchange

SecondariesMatch is a matching and workflow platform, not a national securities exchange or alternative trading system (ATS) unless and until separately registered and disclosed as such.

5. No SIPC / FDIC Coverage

Interests transacted through the platform are not deposits, are not FDIC insured, and are not covered by SIPC protection. Private secondary transactions carry the risks described in our Risk Disclosures.
